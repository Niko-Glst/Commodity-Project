# Gold Desk Annual Review 2026 (year to 20 Nov)

A 13-page portrait review. Its layout follows a long-form annual gold report (cover, contents, key takeaways, margin notes, charts in boxes). Branding is the desk's own. The full-year edition follows after the 31 December close.

Chapters cover:
- the status quo of gold, in seven currencies;
- gold against stocks and silver;
- the macro backdrop;
- demand, flows and positioning (WGC Q3 2026, GLD, CFTC);
- the March and November sell-offs against 2008 and 2020;
- the driver model;
- the In Gold We Trust 2026 theses checked against the data;
- the desk model's year and every test run in 2026;
- an outlook with scenarios and signposts.

- `gold_annual_review_2026.pdf`: the review
- `annual.json`: the numbers computed here; the rest comes from `../2026-11-20-weekly/drivers.json` and `week.json`, `../2026-11-20/results.json` and `../2026-11-19-cycles/cycles.json`
- `scripts/build_annual.py <dir>`: run from `desk/` after the weekly scripts
